AI-assisted Click-to-WhatsApp advertising

I worked on integrating OpenAI APIs with the Meta Ads platform to build an AI-assisted Click-to-WhatsApp advertising workflow inside the Flowcart dashboard:

  1. Merchants enter their advertising requirements as prompts in the dashboard.
  2. OpenAI APIs process the prompt to generate structured information, database queries and campaign data fields.
  3. The client’s product information and images are used to generate advertising creatives.
  4. The generated information is used to prepare the campaign configuration, and the merchant sets the campaign budget.
  5. The workflow creates and publishes the advertisement through Meta Ads.
