How I work

The habits that carry across my roles, from early Android teams to SaaS products.

Building from the ground up

Many of my roles began at an early stage. I built the native Android app and the complete React.js frontend at Sukhiba, built Hoplet’s Android app from scratch, and joined Vrinda Techapps early to help set up its engineering team and processes.

Turning business needs into software

I work directly with founders and business teams to understand requirements, convert them into technical specifications, and define product scope, features and development priorities, as I did as Head of IT at Virtukart.

AI-assisted development, reviewed by me

In recent years I have used AI-assisted development tools such as Cursor to build and maintain commercial products. I review AI-generated code, validate database schemas and application architecture, adapt implementations to the situation, and handle debugging, testing, service integration and production environments myself.

Teams, mentoring and standards

I have run Android training batches, taken part in technical recruitment, built a five-person engineering team from scratch, and mentored junior developers on coding standards, clean code and maintainable development approaches.
