Flowcart (formerly Sukhiba)

WhatsApp commerce SaaS for businesses in Africa and the Middle East

2022 – presentFull-time role

Sukhiba started as a Kenya-focused social-commerce and group-buying app for FMCG goods, groceries and daily essentials. It became Flowcart, a WhatsApp commerce SaaS platform with catalog and order management, broadcasts, automation flows, analytics and ad campaigns. Its merchants were first in Kenya, then in other African markets and the Middle East, including Dubai and Oman.

My contributions

  • Built the original native Android app in Java for the group-buying platform.
  • Architected and developed the complete React.js frontend from scratch, including merchant dashboards, complex business management interfaces and reusable components.
  • Built product catalog, inventory and order management features.
  • Built WhatsApp broadcasts and message templates for simple text, rich media and carousels.
  • Built visual flow builders and automation interfaces.
  • Developed commerce analytics and reporting, QR code generation and sales-team tracking.
  • Integrated the frontend with backend APIs and multiple third-party services.

AI-assisted Click-to-WhatsApp ads

I worked on integrating OpenAI APIs with the Meta Ads platform for this workflow:

  1. The client provides a prompt describing their advertising requirements in the Flowcart dashboard.
  2. The workflow generates campaign details: structured information, database queries and campaign data fields.
  3. Product information and images are used to generate ad creatives.
  4. The client sets the campaign budget.
  5. The campaign is created and published through Meta Ads.

Technologies

  • React.js
  • Java
  • Android
  • Python (basic)
  • OpenAI APIs
  • Meta Ads Platform
  • WhatsApp Business API
  • REST APIs

Rivako

Instagram automation SaaS

Associated with Virtukart TechnologyBuilt independently

Rivako is a commercial Instagram automation platform associated with Virtukart. I designed, developed, launched and maintain it independently, without other developers. It is live and has more than 180 active clients, most of them on the free tier.

What I built

  • The web application in React.js and the landing pages in Next.js.
  • The backend in Node.js, with MongoDB.
  • Razorpay payment gateway integration.
  • Firebase hosting for the frontend and landing pages, and backend infrastructure on Google Cloud Platform.

How it was built

  • AI-assisted development with Cursor.
  • I reviewed AI-generated code, database schemas and application architecture, and changed implementations to fit the requirements.
  • I handle testing, debugging, deployment and production maintenance.

Status: Live, with 180+ active clients, most on the free tier.

LocalPulse

AI-powered Google Business Profile management app

React NativeSole developer

LocalPulse helps businesses manage their Google Business Profile reviews, reputation and marketing. I developed it independently in React Native using AI-assisted development with Cursor, without other developers.

Features

  • AI-generated responses to Google reviews.
  • AI-assisted Google Business Profile post generation.
  • Keyword ranking monitoring and insights.
  • Monthly Google review analytics and reports.
  • Credit-based monetization, with credits bought through Razorpay.

Integrations

  • Supabase
  • Twilio for SMS one-time-password (OTP) verification.
  • Firebase
  • Razorpay for credit purchases.

Status as of Nov 2, 2026: live on Google Play; the iOS version was submitted to the Apple App Store and under review.

Technologies

  • React Native
  • Supabase
  • Twilio
  • Firebase
  • Razorpay
